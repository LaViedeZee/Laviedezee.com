This guide is here to help you serve looks while serving balls. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Padel_The_Basics"></span>Padel: The Basics <span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Before we get into crafting a killer Padel outfit, let’s cover the basics of Padel. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Padel is like the love child of tennis and squash. Rumor has it that the inventor of Padel, Enrique Corcuera, retrofitted his private squash court in Acapulco to accommodate the outdoor fun of tennis in a way that his aging body could participate in with more ease. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Padel racket (or club, or paddle) is somewhere between a pickleball paddle and a tennis racquet. Its large foam face is integrated with<strong> holes </strong>which allow it to move through the air. A welcome difference compared to a pickleball paddle is the much quieter contact it makes with the ball with each stroke. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The rules are very similar to tennis, except for a few significant differences:</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="The_Padel_Serve"></span>The Padel Serve<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The serve in Padel is underhand, versus the overhand serve found in tennis. For those prone to shoulder injuries, this is a huge bonus. </p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="The_Padel_Court"></span>The Padel Court<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Padel courts are smaller than tennis courts. This means a bit less running, but don’t let that fool you. You are now responsible for covering those “wall balls.” The courts are also less hard than traditional American Hard Courts. This means a lot less wear and tear on the body. </p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="No_Singles_in_Padel"></span>No Singles in Padel<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Although there are dedicated singles courts, the average Padel court you will come across will be configured for doubles play. This differs from tennis, in which courts can be used for either doubles or singles play. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Padel-Ready_Outfits"></span>Padel-Ready Outfits<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
