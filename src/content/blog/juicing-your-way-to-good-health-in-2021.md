I would hang around the kitchen waiting for my small glass of juice, the brightly-colored liquid shining like a jewel. My mom had one of the old-school Jack La Lanne juicers. I still remember how she taught us how to clean the parts. You had to rinse everything immediately to keep the bits of plant fibers from sticking to the components like concrete.</p>

The focal point of my abode was the juicer.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Health Benefits</h2>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">Like many others, I have found regular juicing to have an incredibly positive impact on my health. After spending a summer at home juicing carrots almost each day, my eye doctor told me that my vision had improved during my annual vision test, despite the fact that I&#8217;d just finished two years of law school. I mentioned that I had been religiously drinking carrot juice, and he said that it had probably contributed to my improved eye health. </p>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">(If you are considering embarking on a juicing regimen for health reasons, it is of course advisable to consult with your doctor.)</p>
