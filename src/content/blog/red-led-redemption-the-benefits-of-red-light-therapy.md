<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="What_is_Red_Light_Therapy"></span>What is Red Light Therapy?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Red light therapy describes the process of subjecting the body to red and infra-red light to induce beneficial cellular activity. Studies have shown that low-intensity red and near-infrared light stimulates cellular metabolism via a primary photoacceptor: cytochrome C oxidase (<a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2996814/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">source</a>). Cytochrome C oxidase is the terminal enzyme of the mitochondrial electron transport chain. And as we all know from Biology class, the mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell 😌. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The red and near-infrared light spurs activity in the cells. This means enhanced cell turnover as well as elastin and collagen production, giving us the effect of healthier, more youthful skin. This also means enhanced healing of scar tissue. Beyond the skin, red light therapy has been found beneficial for everything from muscle recovery to hair growth. In laser form, red light has also been found to be effective in killing cancer cells without harming the surrounding tissue (<a href="https://www.umassmed.edu/news/news-archives/2014/10/tuning-light-to-kill-deep-cancer-tumors/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">source</a>). </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Does_The_Type_of_Light_Matter"></span>Does The Type of Light Matter?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Studies have shown that a combination of red and near-infrared is the most effective. However, red light alone did also produce beneficial results in some clinical trials. If your LED room lights are all you have access to, they probably won’t be strong enough.  Those light strips don’t have the recommended nanometers (approx 600-900) and won’t be as potent as a LED lamp.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Can_Red_Light_Be_Harmful"></span>Can Red Light Be Harmful?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Some LED lamps caution that overexposure to the light can lead to skin damage. Additionally, those with eye diseases involving the retina of the eye, including diabetes, are also discouraged from using red light therapy (<a href="https://www.news-medical.net/health/Light-Therapy-Safety-and-Side-Effects.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">source</a>).
