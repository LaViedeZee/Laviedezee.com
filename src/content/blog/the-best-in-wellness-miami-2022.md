<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Dr_Smood_Casual_%E2%80%93_Sunset_Harbor_Wynwood_Brickell"></span>Dr. Smood (Casual &#8211; Sunset Harbor, Wynwood &amp; Brickell)<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>

Splurge and try the Hot Virgin Cacao drink with Resveritrol for $20. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Walk_around_Brickell_Key"></span>Walk around Brickell Key<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>

Do a few laps while checking out the super yachts sailing in adjacent Biscayne Bay. Reward yourself with a treat from one of the food trucks right across the river at Brickell Soul.
