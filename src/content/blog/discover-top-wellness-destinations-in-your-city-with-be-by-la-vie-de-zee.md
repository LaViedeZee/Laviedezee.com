<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="For_Wellness-Oriented_Locals_Visitors_Alike"></span>For Wellness-Oriented Locals &amp; Visitors Alike<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Time is our greatest asset—use yours efficiently! Whether it’s a work conference, a family vacation or a doctor’s appointment in a new part of town, the BE by La Vie de Zee Guides connect you with resources to help you stay in alignment with your goals. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Curate_Your_Wellness_Lifestyle_Via_Our_Proprietary_Discovery_Tools"></span>Curate Your Wellness Lifestyle Via Our Proprietary Discovery Tools<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Recommendations in a vacuum rarely produce action. How often have you seen a great studio or restaurant on social media, made a mental note to try it and then just…forgotten to do so? Information without curation is just noise. Energy without direction dissipates. Our Discovery Tools help you filter results based on the factors you care about, such as amenities, location, dietary restrictions, and more. This process removes friction, increasing the likelihood that you’ll gather up the momentum to take action. We also include a helpful FAQ section at the end of each guide containing tips from our lived experience. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Why_BE_by_La_Vie_de_Zee"></span>Why BE by La Vie de Zee?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“BE” is short for “bien-être,” which means <em>wellbeing</em> in French. “La Vie de Zee” is the brand upon which the guides were built: a play on my nickname “Zee” and the original blog spot blog which bore its name—one which featured posts from my study abroad stint in Paris, France. The name is both a play on the Francophile roots of La Vie de Zee as well as a gentle reminder to prioritize your holistic health.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Create_a_Culture_Around_Wellness_Not_Just_Habits"></span>Create a Culture Around Wellness, Not Just Habits<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When I started La Vie de Zee back in 2020, I was looking to make major changes to my health after a serious medical scare and subsequent hospital stay. I knew that simply joining a gym wasn’t going to do the trick; I needed a complete habit overhaul—changing the way that I thought, ate, moved, and even saw the world.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The reason so many people struggle is that they try to make changes in a vacuum, without adequate support, community, and education to achieve significant results over time. A few years later, with 80 pounds down and now a certified Pilates instructor, I can directly attribute much of my success to “being in the room”—connecting myself with people, places, and things that were already aligned with practices surrounding health and wellbeing.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The BE by La Vie de Zee Guides are here to help you plug into a curated “web” of support, filled with services, experts, and tools to guide you along your own wellness journey. Choose what works for you. The most important thing is to start. The second most important thing is to keep going.</p>
