<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Set_the_Mood_Light_a_Candle"></span>Set the Mood, Light a Candle!<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">Now more than ever, creating a comfortable home environment is key. To achieve maximum hygge, I like to evoke all 5 senses. For smell, I turn to my favorite natural wax candles.  A luxe and bougie bougie (&#8220;bougie&#8221; means candle in french) is a great way to imbue a space with an Insta-worthy #aesthetic and an elegant, calming scent. In addition to delivering lovely fragrances, scented candles are a symbol of light, warmth, and hope. </p>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">I used to purchase candles from some of the more popular high-end candle brands until I learned more about paraffin wax. Paraffin wax is a petrochemical byproduct. It is derived from the solids left over during the process of refining crude oil. This waxy residue is treated with chemicals to create paraffin wax.  <a href="http://www.scsu.edu/news_article.aspx?news_id=832" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Studies</a> have shown that burning paraffin wax candles in an enclosed space can be equivalent to releasing diesel exhaust. So&#8230;not super relaxing <em>or</em> healthy. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="The_Search_for_Natural_Wax_Candles"></span>The Search for Natural Wax Candles<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">I started to seriously look into natural wax candle alternatives after giving a close friend an adorable pine-scented mini candle for Christmas. I purchased it from a famous paraffin-friendly candle brand, and I had misgivings afterwards. Going forward, I decided to gift candles that will enhance the lives of my friends, family and colleagues, rather than ones that could be potentially detrimental to their health.</p>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">Luckily, there are incredible natural wax candles on the market. These paraffin-free candles do not compromise in terms of the well-crafted, sumptuous scents and cute branding we have come to expect from prestige candles.</p>
