<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">After a long winter, shoots and buds are beginning to emerge. Springtime means renewal, perfect for those of us looking for a fresh start. With the pandemic still in play, close proximity to others remains ill-advised. However, a new scent could be just what the doctor ordered for your own happiness, even if no one beyond your quarantine bubble gets to smell it. Natural fragrances have come a long way and now rival the most elite of traditional perfumes in both quality and cool factor. </p>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">I have compiled a list of 6 luxury natural fragrances perfect for evoking the spirit of spring. These fragrances were formulated using naturally-derived ingredients, so you can feel confident putting them on your skin. Chemicals in many traditional perfumes have been linked to hormone disruption, reproductive complications, and even cancer (to read more, <a href="https://www.madesafe.org/finding-safer-fragrance/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">click here</a>).  The fragrances on this list have been carefully vetted in accordance with clean beauty retailer Credo Beauty&#8217;s stringent standards (to learn more, <a href="https://credobeauty.com/pages/the-credo-clean-standard-1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">click here</a>). </p>
