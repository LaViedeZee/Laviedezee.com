<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Shilajit_Origin_Story"></span>Shilajit Origin Story<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">Shilajit (also known in the north of India as&nbsp;<em>salajit</em>,&nbsp;<em>shilajatu</em>,&nbsp;<em>mimie</em>, or&nbsp;<em>mummiyo</em>) is a superfood with ancient origins. Shilajit has been used as part of Ayurveda for centuries as a health and anti-aging supplement. It is traditionally gathered from the Himalayan mountains, although you can also find Andean and Siberian versions.  Shilajit is comprised of decomposed plant resin which has been excreted from high mountain ranges. High in fulvic acid, over time it forms an intricate molecular structure encompassing vital trace minerals such as iron, zinc, magnesium, potassium and vitamin B complex. Scientific studies have shown that shilajit can be helpful in slowing cognitive decline (<a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3296184/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">link</a>). </p>
