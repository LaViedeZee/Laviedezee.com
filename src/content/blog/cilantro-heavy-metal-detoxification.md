<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Why_Should_We_Care_About_Heavy_Metals"></span>Why Should We Care About Heavy Metals?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Before we get how cilantro can help the body purge itself of heavy metals, let’s discuss what heavy metals are and why we should care if we’ve been exposed. Heavy metals are naturally occurring elements that can accumulate in the body over time. Exposure to heavy metals occurs through exposure to pollution, contaminated food and water, and even certain medical procedures. The buildup of heavy metals can be toxic to the body. Over time, excess exposure can lead to a range of health problems.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Heavy metals are naturally occurring elements but certain ones wreak havoc on our bodies. They can be found in the environment, in food, and in everyday products. Some heavy metals like iron and zinc are necessary for good health. Others, such as lead, mercury, and arsenic, are extremely toxic. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Sources_of_Heavy_Metal_Exposure"></span>Sources of Heavy Metal Exposure<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As Miranda mentioned in her interview we are all exposed to heavy metals on a pretty regular basis. Here are some of the most common sources of heavy metal exposure:</p>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Food and Water: Heavy metals can enter the food chain through contaminated soil and water. Fish, shellfish, and other seafood are particularly prone to mercury contamination. Lead and cadmium can be found in fruits, vegetables, grains, and nuts.</li>
<li>Industrial Factors: Heavy metals are often used in manufacturing processes. They are released into the environment through air and water pollution. Industrial workers, as well as those living near industrial areas, are at a higher risk of exposure.</li>
<li>Consumer Products: Heavy metals can be found in many consumer products, including cosmetics, and jewelry. Women tend to apply  and wear more consumer products than men on average, making them more prone to exposure. </li>
<li>Medical Treatments: Some medical treatments, such as chemotherapy and radiation, can lead to heavy metal exposure. Some dental procedures may involve mercury-containing dental fillings.</li>
</ol>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Health_Effects_of_Heavy_Metal_Exposure"></span>Health Effects of Heavy Metal Exposure<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While the health effects of daily heavy metal exposure can vary depending on the type of metal and the level of exposure, there are several common health problems that can occur as a result of chronic heavy metal exposure.</p>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Cardiovascular Complications: Lead and cadmium have been linked to an increased risk of heart disease and high blood pressure. Scientists speculate that these metals damage blood vessels and disrupt the normal functioning of the cardiovascular system.</li>
<li>Neurological Issues: Lead and mercury can damage the nervous system, leading to problems with memory, attention, and coordination. In severe cases, heavy metal exposure can cause seizures, coma, and even death.</li>
<li>Kidney Damage: Heavy metals can accumulate in the kidneys, causing damage to these important organs. This can lead to problems with fluid and electrolyte balance, as well as a decrease in kidney function.</li>
<li>Gestational Risks: Exposure during pregnancy can have a range of negative effects on fetal development, including cognitive and behavioral problems.</li>
<li>Cancer: Arsenic and cadmium have been linked to an increased risk of cancer. These metals have been linked to DNA damage and the disruption of proper cell function.</li>
</ol>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Protecting_Yourself_from_Heavy_Metal_Exposure"></span>Protecting Yourself from Heavy Metal Exposure<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It is impossible to completely eliminate your exposure to heavy metals. However, there are several steps you can take to protect yourself from these toxins:</p>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Eat a Healthy Diet: Stack your plate with rich, whole, nutrient-dense foods.  Our bodies have amazing natural detoxification processes, and high-quality foods support the functioning of these processes. Choose organic whenever possible, and limit your intake of seafood that is high in mercury.</li>
<li>Use Non-Toxic Products: Avoid consumer products that are known to contain heavy metals, such as lead-containing jewelry and cosmetics.</li>
<li>Keep an Eye Out for Recalls: Occasionally, it is revealed that even organic or otherwise healthy products have unsafe levels of heavy metals. Keep an eye out for any notices posted about unsafe levels found in your favorite products. </li>
<li>Reduce Exposure to Industrial Pollution: If you are able, select areas to live and spend time that are far from industrial sites or even highways. Areas in close proximity to highways and freeways have been found to be subject to higher levels of heavy metal pollution. </li>
</ol>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="How_Cilantro_Helps_Detoxify_Heavy_Metals"></span>How Cilantro Helps Detoxify Heavy Metals<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Cilantro contains compounds that have been shown to have chelating properties, which means they can bind to heavy metals in the body. The chelation process makes it easier for the body to remove the heavy metals.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The chelating properties of cilantro are due to the presence of a compound called coriandrin. Coriandrin has been shown in studies to have the ability to bind to heavy metals and help to remove them from the body. Cilantro also contains compounds such as flavonoids and phenolic acids, which also have chelating properties.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When consumed, cilantro releases these chelating compounds into the bloodstream. These compounds then bind to heavy metals and make it easier for them to be removed from the body.</p>

By consuming foods rich in antioxidants, such as cilantro, we can help to prevent this damage from occurring and support our overall health.</p>
