<div class="cooked-recipe-directions"><div id="cooked-single-direction-step-1" class="cooked-single-direction cooked-direction cooked-direction-has-number" data-step="Step 1"><span class="cooked-direction-number">1</span><div class="cooked-dir-content"><p>Soak the cashews in water overnight or at least 5 hours before.</p>
</div></div><div id="cooked-single-direction-step-2" class="cooked-single-direction cooked-direction cooked-direction-has-number" data-step="Step 2"><span class="cooked-direction-number">2</span><div class="cooked-dir-content"><p>Preheat oven to 425 degrees.</p>
</div></div><div id="cooked-single-direction-step-3" class="cooked-single-direction cooked-direction cooked-direction-has-number" data-step="Step 3"><span class="cooked-direction-number">3</span><div class="cooked-dir-content"><p>Slice the cauliflower into "steaks" approximately 1 inch thick. Coat each side with olive oil and top with salt and pepper. Red pepper flakes optional for additional spice.</p>
<p>Place "steaks" on oven-safe roasting pan and cook on each side, flipping after approximately 10-15 minutes. </p>
</div></div><div id="cooked-single-direction-step-4" class="cooked-single-direction cooked-direction cooked-direction-has-number" data-step="Step 4"><span class="cooked-direction-number">4</span><div class="cooked-dir-content"><p>Rise the cashews and put them in a high-powered blender or food processor. Add turmeric and the rest of the ingredients for the cashew cream. Blend on high until all ingredients are thoroughly combined. Add water slowly and gradually if a thinner consistency is desired. </p>
</div></div><div id="cooked-single-direction-step-5" class="cooked-single-direction cooked-direction cooked-direction-has-number" data-step="Step 5"><span class="cooked-direction-number">5</span><div class="cooked-dir-content"><p>Heat EVOO in a pan at medium heat. Add chopped rainbow chard and sauté until soft. Salt to taste. </p>
</div></div><div id="cooked-single-direction-step-6" class="cooked-single-direction cooked-direction cooked-direction-has-number" data-step="Step 6"><span class="cooked-direction-number">6</span><div class="cooked-dir-content"><p>Slice Nardello pepper or another mild pepper for garnish to add a pop of fall color. Top steaks with cashew cream and slice peppers. Plate with sautéed chard and enjoy!</p>
