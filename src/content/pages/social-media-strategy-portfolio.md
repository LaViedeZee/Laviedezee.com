<h2 class="wp-block-heading">INTRODUCTION</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The following examples are based upon recent work completed on behalf of a Fortune 100 consumer goods company serving millennial women in a primarily retail-based environment. Because the original work is still pre-production, I have created derivative works here to protect client integrity. </p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading">The Brief</h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The launch of <strong>The Diaspora Suite</strong>—a prestige collection of sensory home and body products. Each scent </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">profile is curated using rare, ethically sourced ingredients from around the world (<em>e.g., Casamance Oud, Oaxaca Vetiver, Bahia Flora</em>).</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The strategic goal is to position this collection not just as a product, but as an&nbsp;<strong>internal sanctuary</strong>. The creative concept centers on&nbsp;<strong>&#8220;The Sacred Pause&#8221;</strong>—the luxury of choosing to mentally travel and find stillness, right where you are.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading">The Target Audience (The Upscale Culturati)</h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Primary:</strong>&nbsp;African American and Hispanic Millennial Women (Ages 30–45).</li>
<li><strong>Geographic Focus:</strong> Highly concentrated in affluent, culturally rich Eastern hubs (e.g., Atlanta, NYC,  Houston, Miami, Charlotte, Philadelphia, Dallas).</li>
<li><strong>Audience Profile:</strong> High-earning professional, entrepreneur, or creative leader. Highly cultured, invests heavily in her home&#8217;s aesthetic, and curated wellness isn&#8217;t a trend for her—it’s a non-negotiable lifestyle. Appreciates subtle nods to heritage, luxury storytelling, and high-end design.</li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Product Modalities (The Prestige Suite)</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Diaspora Suite delivers the fragrance experience through three distinct, premium modalities that must be woven into the social narrative:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>The Room Ritual (Luxury Room Spray &amp; Diffuser Oil):</strong>&nbsp;For instant ambient transformation. The &#8220;pre-hosting&#8221; or morning reset product.</li>
<li><strong>The Body Crème (Ultra-Rich Hydration):</strong>&nbsp;A sensory, skin-softening luxury applied post-bath. The evening wind-down ritual.</li>
<li><strong>The Sensory Candle (Hand-Poured Soy/Coconut Wax):</strong>&nbsp;The ultimate centerpiece for mindfulness, meditation, or a sacred bath.</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Social Strategy Mandate</h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your presentation needs to map out an elevated social approach addressing:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>What does &#8220;mental travel&#8221; look like on TikTok and Instagram?</li>
<li> How do we create content that speaks to the busy, affluent millennial woman&#8217;s desire for soft living and boundaries?</li>
</ul>
