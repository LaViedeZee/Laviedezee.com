🌿Welcome to Your Personal Wellness Operating System, Powered by La Vie de Zee.

Exercise stewardship over your life by enhancing your health, organizing your efforts, and serving from a place of vibrant abundance. This Operating System is engineered to help you become the architect of your wellness lifestyle. By guiding you to state your objectives, clarify your approach, and refine your inputs, this space is where discipline transforms into capacity.

This is a faith-led process created through a Christian framework, predicated on the idea of enlisting divine help to assist you in achieving your goals. Pray, meditate, or sit in silence before engaging with this tool and ask God to help you reach your goals.

Getting Started

Program Overview

How this Operating System works

🛣️ Roadmap: The goal of the Wellness Operating System is to move you through each stage necessary to achieve your wellness goals, as modeled by a Biblical framework. You will progress through three primary phases: ideation, actualization, and tracking. You will begin by clarifying your vision and objectives, proceed to planning and executing your wellness action steps, and conclude by monitoring your progress.

⏲️ Time Investment: Expect to invest 3–5 minutes per day initializing your layout in the morning and closing out your audit metrics at night.

📲 Mobile Execution: For maximum agility, bookmark this page to your phone’s home screen for one-tap access on the go, and enable notifications for anything you schedule elsewhere as reminders.

Before You Begin

Instructions

This Wellness Operating System is more than a mere organizational system. It is meant to act as a virtual Tabernacle, allowing you to move closer to God so that He can hear your prayers. Before we approach God with our requests, it is beneficial to align with His will and be open to His intercession.

The Sauce 🥫: How to Get Closer to God
  1. Cleanse yourself of outward, worldly sin. This can include low-quality media consumption, bad company, gossip, etc. As you spend more time in prayer, aligned action and scripture study, your discernment will improve, and it will become easier to notice unaligned elements in your life.
  2. Offer up something you engage in as a result of indwelling sin. These are the things that come from within us that block our connection with God. God asks us to love Him with all of our heart, all of our soul and all of our strength (Deuteronomy 6:5, Mark 12:30, Luke 10:27). This step requires radical honesty and a functional time audit. Some common examples include giving up emotional coping habits like excess sugar, excess social media use, or restrictive eating. These habits fill a void in our heart, so it’s necessary to ask Jesus to come and fill that void instead for true healing, rather than trying to white-knuckle through it alone.
  3. Accept the power you have to connect with God via your intuition and discernment, as well as the power to resist against the forces of evil. You’re not alone in your journey to create change — Jesus is with you at all times (Matthew 28:20). Make sure to call on Him.
  4. Take aligned action towards what is revealed to you. More on this below.
