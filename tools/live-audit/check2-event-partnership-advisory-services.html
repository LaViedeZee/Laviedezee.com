Advisory Objectives

These packages are designed to provide you with the practical tools and support to host your own profitable and sold-out events. Sessions are structured to provide a clear framework for hosting events in collaboration with venues and brands, from ideation to execution, follow up and community development.  

Quantitative

If the event optics and social media coverage are the leaves and fruit of the tree, the behind-the-scenes work is the roots.

Before getting into event hosting, I was a lawyer. And before that, I was a marketing “nepo baby” (my mother owned her own marketing firm). I’ve drawn extensively on my background—from drafting ad hoc partnership agreements with brands to crafting the language around ticket sales and usage rights. A few examples of what we’ll cover:

  • Business basics: protecting your brand
  • Negotiating with partner brands, including payment terms, usage rights, and deliverables
  • Logistical considerations (COIs, tracking packages, partnering with venues, budgeting, etc.)

Qualitative

Emotional intelligence is extremely important in the events business. Having a good reputation built on strong relationships is key. This is achieved by performing the upfront work to demonstrate your commitment to a successful event and maintaining healthy relationships after the event is over. 

  • Curating the ‘look and feel’ of your brand
  • Communication (emails, video calls, pitch decks, social media content, etc.)
  • Ongoing community cultivation and maintenance (regular contact in your brand voice, a strong POV and a clear sense of what your community can expect to feel as member).
