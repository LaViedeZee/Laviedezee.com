Welcome

Welcome to The BE by La Vie de Zee Miami Recovery Guide. Here, you’ll find a curation of some of the best recovery protocols Miami has to offer. “BE” is short for “bien-être,” which means wellbeing in French. It is both a play on the Francophile roots of La Vie de Zee as well as a gentle reminder to prioritize your holistic health.

Introduction

We take diligent care of our homes, our cars—why not give our bodies that same level of care? After all, they are the sacred vessels that carry us through our journey here on earth. Whether you self-identify as a “biohacking bro,” “wellness girlie,” or just someone wanting to give their body the performance-enhancing TLC it deserves, you’ll find plentiful options below by utilizing our Recovery Discovery Tool.

Miami Recovery: Frequently Asked Questions

What’s an affordable option for trying out different recovery modalities in Miami?

A: You can occasionally find great credit-redemption deals on ClassPass under their “Wellness” section. If you like a certain modality, it’s always worth speaking with the business directly to see if they offer any package discounts on treatments or sessions, which typically yields the best overall price.

Thoughts on lymphatic drainage massage? Any places you would recommend in Miami?

A: My go-to has always been The Shape Spa in Miami Beach. Their practitioners use the Brazilian method, and do a mix of wood tools and cupping along with high-tech add ons like radio frequency for skin tightening. When I first began working out regularly, it was a great way to get a sense of my “progress” by using the massages to encourage my body to flush out the excess water retention.

What’s the most luxe option for recovery treatments?

The Well is a great, reputable brand—they have a membership-only wellness treatment facility as part of their Bay Harbor Island development. They also have a Coconut Grove location opening soon that will be accessible by the general public.
