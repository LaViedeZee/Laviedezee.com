Miami offers premium options for clean makeup application and non-toxic retail cosmetics, ranging from highly specialized luxury glam studios to vetted clean-beauty retail shelves.

Gee Beauty (Sunset Harbor)

A premier celebrity-favorited studio in Miami Beach specializing in high-tech, results-driven makeup applications. They center their application around their own proprietary Gee Beauty makeup line, built specifically to deliver an effortless, skin-safe, everyday glow without heavy synthetic additives.

Minimal Beauty Skincare (Miami Beach)

An elite holistic skincare clinic that provides skin-conscious event makeup application. They work strictly with fully vetted, organic, and clean-certified makeup formulas to guarantee your pores stay clear and free of toxic chemical irritation during your application.

Bluemercury (Coconut Grove)

A boutique environment that features an extensively curated, physical “Clean at Bluemercury” cosmetic section. It is the best destination in Miami to browse and buy premium, non-toxic cosmetic brands like RMS Beauty, ILIA, and Chantecaille in person.

Sephora (Brickell, Miami Beach, South Miami)

Perfect for a quick restock of popular clean cosmetic mainstays. Their physical “Clean at Sephora” displays enforce strict formulation standards, verifying that products are 100% free of harmful parabens, sulfates, and hidden synthetic phthalates.

Wairua Beauty (Aventura, Miami Beach)

While highly praised for their holistic facials, their storefronts double as fully curated clean-luxury cosmetics boutiques. They stock elite, hard-to-find international non-toxic makeup brands alongside clean skin treatments.

Miami features a premier selection of clean facialists and skin studios that focus exclusively on non-toxic, holistic, and plant-based dermal therapies.

Sana Skin Studio (Aventura, Coconut Grove, Wynwood)

A highly popular, membership-backed clean skin movement. They redefine facials by using strictly non-toxic, goal-driven ingredients paired with client education. Their signature facial pathways treat the skin safely without standard synthetic fillers.

Wairua Beauty (Aventura, Miami Beach)

One of Miami’s top skincare studios specializing in clean beauty brands. Avail yourself of one of their decadent treatments for glowing, hydrated skin.

Daily Habits Wellness Club (Coral Gables)

A curated lifestyle lounge blending high-performance botanical facial sculpting with clean beauty science. They offer specialized treatments like non-toxic buccal facial massages, gua sha, and holistic therapies geared toward resetting irritated skin layers.

ECO Skin Studio (North Miami)

A dedicated facial center built on the baseline foundation that optimal beauty should not damage your body or the ecosystem. They formulate their targeted protocols around certified organic, raw botanical skincare solutions.

Miami Natural Skincare (Miami Beach)

An organic day spa that provides highly customized dermal exfoliation, microneedling, and oxygen therapies utilizing cleanly formulated botanical products.

Miami offers a fantastic selection of clean, sunless tanning boutiques and luxury airbrush services that exclusively utilize eco-certified DHA, plant-based botanicals, and vegan formulas to deliver a streak-free, natural glow without exposure to harsh synthetic dyes, parabens, or typical self-tanner odors.

SUGARED + BRONZED (Midtown Miami)

A premier sunless destination famous for its proprietary, rapid-drying FlexExpress® sunless airbrush solution. Their service is engineered to produce a completely streak-free, ultra-natural faux glow that develops rapidly and fades evenly. They also specialize in all-natural sugaring hair removal using just sugar, lemon, and water.

Ta-Tan! Miami (Midtown)

A top-tier luxury sunless brand praised for custom-blended, hyper-natural airbrush coloring built for models, celebrities, and events. They use a specialized pH-balancing skin primer alongside rapid-rinsing, quick-drying organic ingredients to ensure maximum color longevity.

Glow Up Miami (Midtown Miami)

A highly rated, modern boutique studio known for its cult-favorite, 100% vegan, never-orange formula. They specialize in precise custom color-matching and subtle body contouring, offering both clean studio appointments and premium house-call mobile services.

Katie’s by Katie Becker (Brickell)

A private, specialized airbrush studio that focuses exclusively on clean tanning methods—in addition to providing services like eyelash extensions, lash lifts and eyebrow lamination. Clients praise the studio for its fully organic solution bases that leave behind a seamless, subtle sun-kissed finish without any chemical fragrance or skin tightness.

City Glow Florida (Brickell)

A clean beauty oasis in the heart of Brickell. Alongside their botanical facials and lash treatments, they provide certified clean sunless spray tans customized completely to your skin's baseline tone.

Sobe Tan by Fabiola (Miami Beach)

An iconic, long-standing Miami Beach tanning studio that has worked with numerous celebrities. They have strictly committed to using clean, non-toxic sunless products only for their technician-applied custom airbrush sessions and high-end VersaSpa Pro systems.

Best Miami Tan (Coral Gables)

Tucked inside Flawless by Melissa Fox, this private boutique offers meticulously blended airbrush tans by appointment only. They center their application around clean, skin-safe formulations designed to nourish the skin barrier while developing.

Happy Tanning (Coral Gables)

A dedicated beauty and brow spot that provides hand-sprayed technician-applied organic full body spray tans for a long-lasting, seamlessly blended golden hue.

Sun Tan Miami (Coral Gables)

A highly frequented tanning brand whose popular airbrush services utilize 100% organic custom bronzing solutions that develop in minutes without patchy peeling.

True Tan (Coral Gables)

A dedicated, holistic beauty lounge that explicitly uses ethical, vegan, and 100% organic ingredients. Their hand-sprayed custom airbrush services are highly rated for avoiding harsh chemicals and synthetic additives while ensuring a natural bronze.

LIVTan Miami (Brickell)

Operating out of a private Brickell studio as well as a luxury mobile concierge service, they use custom, clean airbrush blends to achieve a completely natural-looking skin tint directly in your home.

Ellie's Beauty Touch (Brickell)

A luxury beauty service provider offering specialized airbrush tanning utilizing 100% organic, botanical-infused, naturally bronzed solution bases with rapid 3-hour rinse options.

Wairua Beauty (Miami Beach)

This studio is a gold standard for avoiding harmful synthetics. Their specialized body contouring and manual lymphatic drainage massages strictly reject standard synthetic massage gels, mineral oils, and synthetic perfumes. Instead, they use pure, cold-pressed botanical lipids and targeted plant extracts that work seamlessly with your skin's biology.

Leaf Spa at AKA Brickell

Their full-body detox therapies explicitly utilize raw ingredients and certified organic botanicals. They completely swap out synthetic foaming agents (like sulfates) and synthetic chemical exfoliants for clean, earth-derived elements like mineral clays and plant-derived enzymes.

Conventional hair coloring, straightening, and styling products are among the most chemically aggressive segments of the beauty industry, frequently relying on ammonia, formaldehyde-releasing ingredients, resorcinol, and synthetic PPDs to force open the hair cuticle. Applying the same rigorous vetting standard used for body care — screening out high-risk synthetic allergens, endocrine disruptors, and toxic fumes — Miami features a top-tier collection of hair wellness centers and organic salons.

The Root & Bark Organic Hair Salon (Downtown Miami)

A highly specialized, intimate eco-luxury hair studio that operates entirely without standard salon chemical fumes. They work exclusively with The Organic Way (Oway), the beauty industry's premier professional styling and color line formulated entirely with biodynamic, organic, and fair-trade botanical actives. Private studio in Miami, plus dedicated residential travel bookings.

Sayblee Holistic HairGrowth Center (MiMo)

Led by a Certified Trichologist (scalp scientist), this clinic specializes in scalp therapy, thinning hair, and restoration. They explicitly reject synthetic texturizers, parabens, and chemical silicones, instead treating hair using their own patented, 100% plant-based, vegan systems like the Rosemary Clarifying and Lime Nourishing Scalp Healing lines.

Ocean Salon by Gabo Melo (Miami Beach)

Steps from Lincoln Road, this completely fume-free and cruelty-free salon specializes in certified organic coloring. For clients seeking sleek styling without exposure to carcinogenic synthetic gases, they utilize an innovative formaldehyde-free, amino-acid-based organic keratin straightening system that eliminates frizz safely.

Alisados Orgánicos Hair Salón (Coral Gables)

A specialized hair boutique focusing heavily on non-toxic texture modification. Their Alisado Orgánico (Organic Straightening) treatments discard conventional synthetic relaxers in favor of 100% plant-derived moisturizing and volume-reducing acid complexes to smooth hair cleanly.

Miami has several highly-rated nail salons that explicitly prioritize non-toxic, healthy, and conscious hand and foot care.

GLOSS Nail Bar (Coral Gables)

A premier completely non-toxic nail salon on Miracle Mile. They formulate and use their own signature line of all-natural scrubs, mists, and polishes. Their lacquers are guaranteed 10-free, vegan, preservative-free, and fragrance-free, making it a perfect toxic-fume-free zone. They also offer long-lasting, healthy Dazzle Dry systems.

LUV BEAUTY CLUB (Midtown Miami)

A modern, conscious luxury beauty club that strictly bans acrylics, dip powders, and heavy chemical products from their salon. They focus entirely on safe, high-quality products and specialized dry Russian manicures that protect and preserve your natural nail health.

Cilverbow Botanicals (Mobile Only)

A boutique concierge-style natural nail service focusing purely on healthy nail architecture and restoration. They manufacture their own brand of biodynamic, plant-infused hand and foot care products that are fully biodegradable and chemical-free. Salon styling in Miami, plus dedicated mobile service options — no fixed address, so it's featured here as a Miami-wide booking.

Nail Patrol Miami (Mobile Only)

A high-end mobile beauty concept that brings safe, non-toxic manicures and pedicures straight to your home, office, or hotel suite. They use highly vetted, clean products and single-use, customized hygiene kits to ensure full consumer safety without chemical contamination. On-demand across the greater Miami region — no fixed address, so it's featured here as a Miami-wide booking.

A note on where this category is headed: Miami's clean beauty scene is still finding its footing compared to more established markets like New York or Los Angeles. You won't yet find an integrated concept store like Credo or CAP Beauty here, and a handful of the studios above operate by appointment only, without a public storefront to list. We see that as a feature of a growing market, not a flaw in this guide: it reflects exactly where Miami's clean beauty movement stands today, and we'll keep updating it as new additions open their doors.

Frequently Asked Questions: Miami Clean Beauty

Where is the best place to get a clean beauty facial in Miami, Florida?

We love Sana Skin Studio, which offers multiple locations across Miami. Our favorite location is in Coconut Grove, as it is near other great neighborhood spots such as Sadelle’s (great for brunch) and Emissary Café. Wairua Beauty is also a highly-ranked spot to try, featuring top clean beauty lines such as Augustinus Bader and Josh Rosebrook.
